Private candidate content for the Suikoden behaviour port.

Original game content belongs to Konami and is taken from the owner's private
disc dump. It is not redistributable, and this package grants no license to it.

This package is a private, local candidate. Review, promotion, publication and
distribution remain separate gates; the runtime only certifies that the files
below match the declared manifest.

Extraction provenance is recorded in the declared provenance documents
(provenance.json and battle/provenance.json). Each source names the disc file,
its LBA and SHA-256, plus the decoder or exporter that produced the bytes.
